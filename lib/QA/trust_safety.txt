4. Trust & Safety (the wedge — nothing here is optional) 
• [x] 
 Checkr (or equivalent) is integrated. Invitation sent from the coach 
funnel, webhook updates background_check_status, human review queue for 
consider results. Manual enum-flipping is not a launch state for a product whose 
one-line pitch is "background-checked coaches." 
Easy: Today "checked" is a hand-flipped label. Not real. Connect a real check 
company. It reports back automatically. Check: Checkr live, auto-update, 
review queue. 
• [x] 
 FCRA adverse action flow. If a check disqualifies a coach: 
pre-adverse-action notice + copy of report + waiting period + final notice. 
Skipping this is statutory damages per violation. Template the emails now. 
Easy: Law: to reject a coach for their record, you must warn, share the report, 
wait, then answer. Skip it = fines. Check: notice emails written. 
• [x] 
 Gating enforced server-side. The client matcher's verified/active/intensity 
gates must mirror a server match_eligible that is the actual authority. Pass: 
manually flip a coach to unverified in DB; they vanish from search, match, 
chat-answers, and direct-link booking simultaneously. 
Easy: Safety rules live on the SERVER, not just the app. Apps can be tricked. 
Check: flip coach off → gone from search, match, chat, links. 
• [x] 
 Recency shown honestly. "Background checked" in UI displays the 
completed-at date only when a real date exists — verify no path shows the badge 
with a null date. 
Easy: Show the real check date. No date = no badge. Check: date shown, no 
empty badges. 
• [x] 
 Report & block. Parent can report a coach and block contact from any chat 
or profile in ≤ 2 taps; report lands somewhere you actually monitor daily. Coach 
can report a parent. 
Easy: Report or block in 2 taps, from anywhere. Reports go where YOU look 
daily. Check: report, block, both sides, checked daily. 
• [x] 
 Incident response SOP (one page, written). Allegation of harm arrives → 
immediate suspension pending review, preservation of all messages/bookings, who 
you call (counsel, and when mandated, authorities), what you say and don't say. 
You must never draft this for the first time during the incident. 
Easy: If someone says a coach hurt a kid: suspend now, save everything, call 
lawyer, know your words. Write the page TODAY. Check: one page, written, 
before launch. 
• [x] 
 ToS honesty about verification. State precisely what a background check 
does and does not guarantee. Overclaiming ("safe," "vetted and trusted") is your 
liability multiplier. 
Easy: Say honestly what a check is and isn't. Never promise "safe." Big words 
hurt you in court. Check: honest wording, no overclaims. 
• [x] 
 Continuous monitoring (Checkr Continuous or scheduled re-checks) — a 
check at onboarding decays. Acceptable to defer past launch only with annual 
re-check policy written into coach ToS from day one. 
Easy: Old check says nothing about today. Best: live monitoring. Minimum: 
yearly re-check in the rules. Check: re-check policy in ToS. 
• [x] 
 Coach safety attestation at onboarding: agrees to observable-session norms 
(parent may watch, public venues, no private transport of minors). Cheap to add, 
meaningful in a deposition. 
Easy: Coach checks a box: parents can watch, public places, never drive kids. 
Costs a checkbox. Matters in court. Check: box added.
