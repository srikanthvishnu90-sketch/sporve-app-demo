# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Families and guardians finding safe, appropriate youth-sports coaching, and
coaches or programs managing listings, bookings, communication, and payments.
Use frequently happens on phones, outdoors, in gyms, and under time pressure.

## Product Purpose

Help a guardian move from an athlete's goal to a trustworthy coach, an accurately
priced booking, accountable payment, and visible progress. Help providers operate
that relationship without inventing credentials, outcomes, availability, or
financial facts.

## Business Model

Sporve is funded by one Free, Pro, or Enterprise subscription per provider
workspace. Sporve does not take a percentage of coaching bookings or invoices.
Stripe processing and connected-account payout costs are separate transaction
facts, and organization-to-trainer revenue splits are compensation rules rather
than Sporve revenue.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, energetic, and trustworthy. Sporve should feel capable and direct,
without sounding institutional, luxurious, clinical, or artificially certain.

## Anti-references

Dark fitness interfaces that sacrifice daylight legibility; gambling-like sports
visuals; generic AI dashboards; fake marketplace abundance; unearned trust
badges; invented legal, safety, response-time, payment, or outcome claims.

## Design Principles

- Trust is evidence: name the specific check, status, source, and date.
- The guardian approves consequential actions; AI proposes and explains.
- Money and availability come from server-owned facts, never display state.
- Fail honestly: distinguish empty, offline, unavailable, and failed states.
- Optimize for field use: reachable controls, readable type, and daylight support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, 44-by-44 logical-pixel interactive targets, screen-reader
semantics, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, text scaling, and
non-color status cues. Youth data must be guardian-controlled and minimized.
